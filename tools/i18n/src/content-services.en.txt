68	Removing a seal placed for a building violation
69	Seal removal procedure
70	Procedure for removing a seal placed for a building violation.
71	Information to include in the application: the applicant's name, the property and full address, a description of the violation and its area, and how long the temporary seal removal is needed.
72	Application: the citizen applies to the relevant service department to have the seal removed.
73	Removal pledge: the application includes a pledge, certified by a notary, to remove the violation on the property.
74	Submit the seal removal application with the pledge to remove the violation.
75	The service department asks the police station to remove the seal temporarily.
76	The seal is removed for a set period only, so the violation can be removed.
77	After removal, the work is checked, a report is drawn up and the seal is put back.
78	A memo is sent to the Governor requesting final removal of the seal.
79	Once approved, the seal is officially removed and a final report is drawn up.
80	Seal removal – restoration without a restoration permit
81	Seal removal procedure – restoration without a restoration permit
82	Pledge: the application includes a pledge, certified by a notary, not to carry out any restoration work without a restoration permit.
83	Information to include in the application: the applicant's name, the property and full address, and the number of the restoration permit application submitted to the department.
84	A notarised pledge not to carry out any restoration work without a restoration permit.
85	Submit the seal removal application together with a restoration permit application.
86	The service department asks the police station to remove the seal temporarily.
87	The seal is removed for a set period only, so the site can be inspected for a restoration permit.
88	A report is drawn up for restoration without a permit, and it is settled in the usual way.
89	A memo is sent to the Governor requesting final removal of the seal, after the restoration permit is obtained and the violation settlement fees are paid.

106	Shared roofs and shared setbacks
107	Procedure for installing solar panels – shared roofs and shared setbacks
108	A request from the administrative committee stating that the work will be done with its knowledge. If there is no committee, a temporary one is formed; if that isn't possible, the administrative unit divides the shared space.
109	Proof of ownership of the shared roof or setback.
110	A sketch of the whole shared area divided among the units entitled to use it, with each unit's number written on its share.
111	Building permit.
112	Conditions: (No system installed on shared property may block access to any part of it for maintenance, or to other systems and equipment on these areas. – The metal frame for the panels must meet structural and public safety standards in its design, fixing and installation, and installing the metal base on the top roof must not damage the roof, especially its waterproofing; tiled sloping roofs require a separate metal frame. – When installing panels on a roof built in violation on the top floor, a proper technical report on the structural safety of that roof must be submitted. – Shared roofs may only be reached through shared property. – The panels must start 50 cm above the equipment on the roof, may not rest on that equipment or obstruct its maintenance. – Keep away from light and ventilation openings; if harm occurs, the matter goes to court. – Install a light at the building entrance.)
